The Origin Story

Built out of sheer frustration with calorie counters.

Musette was created by an athlete who loves cooking, trains 8 to 14 hours a week across running, cycling, and lifting, and got tired of fighting software that treated multi-hour endurance workouts like an error in a spreadsheet.

"If you run 15 miles or ride 50 miles on a Saturday, you don't need a calorie deficit lecture from an algorithm that assumes everyone works in an office cubicle and walks 3,000 steps a day. You need to know how many grams of carbohydrate to eat and what delicious food to cook for dinner."

The Musette Philosophy

Most fitness tech exists to maximize monthly active users, engagement metrics, and recurring subscription fees. Musette is built under a completely different ethos:

1. Output Over Guesswork

Your devices measure real physical energy expenditure. We use metabolic physics rather than generic approximations to calculate exact energy demands.

2. Software as an Instrument

No push notifications, no badges, no streaks. Designed like a well-made chef's knife or a machined stem: precise, quiet, and reliable.

3. Real Food for Real Lives

Nutrition shouldn't mean choking down chalky powders. Musette plans delicious, hearty family meals — smash burgers, slow-cooked ragù, vibrant salmon bowls — with portions calibrated to your workouts.

4. Zero Surveillance

No ad trackers, no SDK telemetry, and no third-party data broker deals. Your health and training data stays isolated and automatically prunes after 90 days.

Why "Musette"?

In endurance sport, a musette is the cotton feed bag handed to athletes mid-effort containing essential food, rice cakes, and hydration. It represents simple, purposeful nourishment when you're working hardest.
