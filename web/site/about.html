The Origin Story

Built out of frustration with TrainingPeaks, Strava, and Garmin.

Musette was built by someone who was frustrated with what TrainingPeaks, Strava, or Garmin could provide and found themselves constantly putting all of their training into AI for further analysis — but also couldn't justify spending hundreds a month on a trainer with limited communication.

"I was tired of staring at platforms that tell you what you did yesterday without telling you how to fuel tomorrow. And paying hundreds a month for a remote coach who only checks in once a week made no sense when AI can analyze metabolic load in seconds and plan real, delicious food."

The Musette Approach

Rather than giving you another isolated chart or charging astronomical monthly coaching retainers, Musette bridges the gap between completed workouts and practical nutrition:

1. Automated AI Training Analysis

Stop copying and pasting workout summaries into chat prompts. Musette ingests your workouts directly from intervals.icu and delivers actionable, daily nutritional targets automatically.

2. Coaching Quality Without the $300/Mo Fee

Instant energy calculations, carbohydrate periodization, and recovery advice whenever you train — without spending hundreds a month on slow communication.

3. Real Food, Calibrated to Workouts

No bland boiled chicken or chalky supplements. Musette plans hearty, delicious meals — smash burgers, slow-cooked ragù, vibrant salmon bowls — calibrated to your actual output.

4. Privacy & Zero Surveillance

No ad trackers, no SDK telemetry, and no third-party data broker deals. Your training metrics stay private and automatically prune after 90 days.

Why "Musette"?

In endurance sport, a musette is the cotton feed bag handed to athletes mid-effort containing essential food, rice cakes, and hydration. It represents simple, purposeful nourishment when you're working hardest.
