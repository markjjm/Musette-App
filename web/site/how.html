Training Measures Output. Energy is the Result.

A power meter, GPS running watch, or heart rate monitor measures physical effort continuously. In endurance exercise, $1\text{ kJ of mechanical work} \approx 1\text{ kcal of metabolic expenditure}$ because human muscular efficiency is approximately 21% to 24%.

Carbohydrate Periodization

Muscle and liver glycogen stores hold approximately 400g to 500g of carbohydrate. An intense multi-hour workout drains those stores nearly dry. Musette periodizes your daily carbohydrate targets to match your actual training load:

Day Type Training Intensity Carb Target (Imperial) During-Workout Fuel Dinner Goal
Rest / Light Recovery Zone 0 – 1 (Easy spin/walk) 1.4 – 1.8 g/lb (3–4 g/kg) Hydration only Pan-Seared Salmon & Quinoa Avocado Bowl
Aerobic Base (1–2h) Zone 2 (Endurance) 2.3 – 3.2 g/lb (5–7 g/kg) 45 – 60 g/h Honey-Ginger Chicken & Jasmine Rice Bowl
Intervals / Hard Tempo Zone 4 – 5 (Threshold) 3.4 – 4.3 g/lb (7.5–9.5 g/kg) 60 – 90 g/h Rigatoni Bolognese with Garlic Sourdough
Long Endurance (3h+) Zone 2 – 3 (Epic volume) 4.5+ g/lb (10+ g/kg) 80 – 100 g/h Smash Burgers on Brioche with Crispy Wedges
