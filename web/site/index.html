How it operates

Three simple steps. Then it runs on autopilot.

1

Connect your device

Link intervals.icu in 30 seconds. Your Garmin, Wahoo, Apple Watch, COROS, or Zwift automatically delivers completed workouts to Musette.

2

Set your weekly rhythm

Configure your weight in lbs, target hours, and training days. The engine computes your metabolic baseline and shifts carb targets dynamically.

3

Cook, eat & recover

A full month of dinner recipes calibrated to your intensity. The grocery list is grouped by aisle so shopping takes minutes.

Our Commitment

Software built like a fine chef's knife.

We don't monetize your screen time, sell your health records, or design habit loops to trap you in an app.

  • No feeds, streaks, or badges. Nothing engineered to trigger dopamine loops or waste your recovery time.
  • No advertising and zero data brokers. Your bodyweight and workout data are strictly confidential and prune after 90 days.
  • No food guilt. No "earning" calories or "burning off" meals. Food is clean performance fuel.
  • Not a medical device. Built on physiological physics and sport science. For medical advice, consult a physician.
  • Invite only. Kept deliberately small so it remains sovereign, fast, and free.
