Device & Training Sync

Musette ingests completed workouts from intervals.icu (which connects directly with Garmin, Wahoo, Apple Watch, COROS, Polar, Suunto, and Zwift).

  1. Open intervals.icu/settings and navigate to Developer Settings.
  2. Copy your API key, and your athlete id from the top of the page (e.g. i123456).
  3. Paste both below. Verified with intervals.icu before storage.

Encrypted with AES-GCM-256 before storage. Disconnect or rotate anytime.
