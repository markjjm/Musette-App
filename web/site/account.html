Wearable & Device Integrations (Garmin, Wahoo, Zwift, Apple Health, COROS)

Musette synchronizes all your multi-sport activities (cycling, running, swimming, strength, tennis, and more) through intervals.icu. Connect once to automatically import workouts from Garmin, Wahoo, Zwift, Rouvy, Hammerhead, COROS, and Apple Health.

Where do I find my API key? (Click to view quick guide)
  1. Open your intervals.icu profile settings (under Developer Settings).
  2. Copy the API Key (a string of letters and numbers).
  3. Copy your Athlete ID at the very top of your intervals.icu settings page (e.g. 123456).
  4. Paste both above and click Connect & Test Synchronization.

Credentials are encrypted with AES-GCM-256 before storage. You can disconnect anytime.
