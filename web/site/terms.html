Terms of Use

Clear and straightforward terms. Musette is free, invite-only, and operated independently for endurance athletes who cook.

Not a Medical Device

Musette is not a medical device and does not provide medical, clinical, or therapeutic advice. It performs physical arithmetic on endurance training work and food data. It is not a substitute for a licensed medical doctor, a registered sports dietitian, or a qualified coach.

Do not use Musette to manage a clinical condition. If you are pregnant, a minor, managing diabetes, cardiac issues, an eating disorder, or any history of disordered eating, please consult a healthcare professional.

What You Agree To

  • Personal Use: One account per individual athlete. Do not resell access or use Musette commercially for coaching clients without authorization.
  • Security Integrity: No automated scraping, denial-of-service attempts, or efforts to access other athletes' data.
  • Understand the Estimates: Nutritional values derive from USDA and reference databases; workout energy comes from power meters, GPS devices, or head unit calculations. Use them as an informed baseline rather than laboratory-grade chemistry.
  • Allergen Responsibility: You remain entirely responsible for what you purchase, cook, and consume, including allergen verification.

What We Agree To

  • Zero Monetization of Your Data: No advertising, no trackers, and no selling or sharing of athlete data.
  • Data Sovereignty: Instant, self-serve JSON export and complete account deletion with no waiting period.
  • Honest Failure: When data is unverified or an external API is down, the app fails clearly and honestly rather than fabricating data.

Contact & Governance

Reach us at hello@musetteapp.com. Governed by the laws of England and Wales.
